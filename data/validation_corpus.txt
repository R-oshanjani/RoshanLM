Subword tokenization is useful because natural language contains a large number of possible words. A tokenizer with only whole-word tokens requires a very large vocabulary. Character tokenization avoids unknown words but produces long sequences. Subword tokenization provides a compromise between vocabulary size and sequence length.

The relationship between tokens, vocabulary size, context length, and model capacity is important. A larger vocabulary can reduce sequence length but increases the number of output classes. A smaller vocabulary produces longer sequences but gives the model fewer output classes. The best choice depends on the corpus and the available training data.
